## Why This Exists / 为什么有这个博客

我不想把经历只留在简历里。简历擅长压缩结果，但博客更适合保留过程、判断、失败、修正与成长。  
I don't want my experience to live only inside a resume. A resume compresses outcomes well, but a blog preserves process, judgment, failure, correction, and growth.
