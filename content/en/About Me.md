# About Me

I'm Dingyi Tian, based in Chengdu, China, working across DevOps, cloud operations, infrastructure automation, CI/CD, observability, and engineering delivery.

I am especially drawn to systems that need to stay reliable over time. I care less about one-off demos and more about whether a system can keep working under collaboration pressure, production complexity, and operational constraints.

I want this site to serve as both a personal profile and a growing knowledge garden. It is where I document my experience, projects, working style, and the writing I plan to build over time.

## What I Do

- Turn infrastructure capability into real delivery
- Balance reliability, speed, and maintainability
- Work through deployment, monitoring, migration, and recovery problems in complex systems
- Use tools, process, and documentation to make teams work better together

## How I Work

- Learn unfamiliar systems quickly and identify key constraints
- Find the real problem inside ambiguous requirements
- Prefer infrastructure that can be maintained for the long term
- Make clear, practical decisions under pressure
