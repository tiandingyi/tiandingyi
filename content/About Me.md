# About Me / 关于我

我叫田丁毅，现在在中国成都，主要从事 DevOps、Cloud Ops、基础设施自动化与工程交付相关工作。  
I'm Dingyi Tian, based in Chengdu, China, working at the intersection of DevOps, cloud operations, infrastructure automation, and engineering delivery.

我偏爱那些“需要长期稳定交付”的系统：它们不能只在 Demo 时成立，而要在多人协作、严格流程、真实流量、复杂依赖和合规环境里持续运转。  
I gravitate toward systems that must survive long-term delivery. Not systems that only look good in a demo, but systems that continue working under collaboration pressure, strict process, real traffic, messy dependencies, and compliance constraints.

## What I Do / 我在做什么

我的工作常常落在这些交叉点上：  
My work usually lives at the intersection of:

- 平台与业务之间的落地能力 / turning platform capability into business delivery
- 基础设施与工程效率之间的平衡 / balancing infrastructure rigor with engineering speed
- 稳定性、可观测性与团队协作 / reliability, observability, and team collaboration
- 从复杂问题里拆出清晰路径 / carving a clear path through complex problems

## How I Work / 我如何工作

我相信好的工程师不只是“会技术”，而是能在复杂约束中稳定交付，并让团队因你的存在而更轻松。  
I believe strong engineers are not defined by tooling alone, but by their ability to deliver under complex constraints and leave the team in a better state.

我在工作里反复训练这几件事：  
I keep training a few things over and over:

- 快速学习陌生系统 / learning unfamiliar systems quickly
- 在模糊需求里定位核心问题 / isolating the real problem inside ambiguity
- 从零搭出能长期维护的基础设施 / building infrastructure that can be maintained over time
- 在压力下做清晰决策 / making clear decisions under pressure
