# Principles / 原则

> [!quote] Motto / 人生格言
> 你越功利，世界对你越神秘。  
> _The more utilitarian your lens, the more mysterious the world becomes._

## What This Means to Me / 这句话对我的意义

这句话并不是反对目标感，而是在提醒我：如果一个人只盯着最短路径、最直接回报、最表面的结果，就会越来越看不见世界内部更深的结构。  
This idea is not anti-ambition. To me, it means that if we only look for the shortest path, the fastest reward, and the most obvious outcome, we gradually lose sight of the deeper structure of the world.

无论是技术还是人生，很多真正重要的东西都需要耐心、好奇心和长期投入才能理解。  
In engineering as in life, many important things can only be understood through patience, curiosity, and long-term commitment.

## Engineering Principles / 工程原则

- 稳定交付比短期炫技更重要  
  Sustainable delivery matters more than flashy short-term wins
- 真正的能力，是在复杂系统里持续做出清晰判断  
  Real ability is the capacity to make clear decisions inside complex systems
- 基础设施不是配角，它决定系统能否长期存在  
  Infrastructure is not a side concern; it shapes whether systems can endure
- 好的工程协作会让团队整体变强  
  Good engineering collaboration makes the whole team stronger
